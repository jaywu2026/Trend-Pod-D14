This Week (Actual) = eComm units already sold this fiscal week; → est. N this wk projects the full week from the day-of-week pattern (eComm skews to Monday — DSV ships Mon), so it runs ahead of a normal week when a big sales day is still coming. Forecast Weekly Rate = the item's average units over past complete selling weeks — the steady baseline; the ▲/▼ next to it compares today's rate to the average of the past 7 days it's been computed. Weeks of Supply = Available ÷ Forecast Weekly Rate; the ▲/▼ is the WOS change per week vs the snapshot ~1 week ago (2026-09-13) — with no replenishment WOS naturally burns −1.0/wk, so a drop steeper than 1 means selling faster than the forecast rate (green when stock is healthy), and a drop on a low-stock item (<8 wks) shows red.
