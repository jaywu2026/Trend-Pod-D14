N this wk projects the full week from the day-of-week pattern (eComm skews to Monday — DSV ships Mon), so it runs ahead of a normal week when a big sales day is still coming. Forecast Weekly Rate = the item's average units over the last 4 complete selling weeks (rolling, so a ramping item isn't dragged down by early slow weeks); the ▲/▼ next to it compares today's rate to the average of the past 7 days it's been computed. Items with under 1 week of true cover get an OOS ×0.1 damped rate (they can't realize the full rate without stock) until replenishment brings cover back above 1 week — their Weeks of Supply uses the damped rate, so the red "true cover" figure next to it is the undamped number. Weeks of Supply = Available ÷ Forecast Weekly Rate; the ▲/▼ is the WOS change per week vs the snapshot ~1 week ago (2026-11-11) — with no replenishment WOS naturally burns −1.0/wk, so a drop steeper than 1 means selling faster than the forecast rate (green when stock is healthy), and a drop on a low-stock item (<8 wks) shows red.
